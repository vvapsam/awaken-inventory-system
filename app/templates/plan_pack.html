Teams of 4, relay format ~30 teams for 120 people Wave / heat starts RX / scaled / walk options

              Format & stations

              Teams of 4 complete the circuit relay-style. Between each station the team runs a short lap together; at each station members share the required reps (e.g. 100 wall balls split across the team). Everyone finishes together. Offer RX / scaled / walk options at every station, and stagger team starts in waves so no station bottlenecks.
