The Reel

Whether this event asks anybody to post. It is the sponsor's side of a sponsored class — somebody comes free and posts about it — so it is off by default on an open event, where people paid to enter and owe nobody a Reel. With it off there is no Reel form on their page, no Reel email, and no reward to pick.

The race app

How early a coach can open a heat on their phone. Before that the heat is listed but shut, so nobody grabs an athlete out of a heat three hours away and holds them there. Once a heat opens it stays open for the rest of the day, however late it runs — a coach shut out of a race already on the floor has no way back to their own athlete.
