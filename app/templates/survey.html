Prize rules and privacy

The prize

  • What — one free 1-year Awaken membership. Transferrable to another person. Not exchangeable for cash.
  • When — entries close {{ closes_long }}. One winner drawn at random and announced by {{ announces_long }}. All times are Manila time.
  • Entries — one per mobile number. Your referral QR earns one extra entry for every friend who completes the survey and qualifies, up to five.
  • One person, one entry — entering more than once using different mobile numbers is not allowed. Duplicate or fraudulent entries, including referring yourself or using numbers you do not hold, may be disqualified along with any other entries connected to them. Awaken’s decision on any disqualification is final.
  • Who can join — adults aged 18 and over living in Metro Manila.
  • Claiming — in person at Awaken Fitness, Pasig City, by the person holding the mobile number on the entry. An entry whose number we cannot verify cannot be claimed, so please enter it carefully.
  • Deadline — claim within 30 days of the announcement, or the prize is forfeited.

Your details

  • What we keep — your nickname, mobile number and email, and your answers. If this round isn’t open to you, we keep only your mobile number and email, so you aren’t asked again.
  • Why — your mobile number ensures each person answers once and is how we contact you if you win. Your email is a secondary contact.
  • Who sees it — Awaken only. We do not sell or share your details.
  • Messages — we will only contact you about this survey and the prize draw, nothing else.
  • How long — your name, number and email are deleted 30 days after the draw. The winner’s details are kept until the prize is claimed or forfeited, then deleted.
  • Your device — limited technical information is recorded solely to identify duplicate entries.
  • Your rights — you may request a copy of your answers, or have them deleted, at any time. Email admin@awakengym.com or ask at the front desk. Handled under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).
