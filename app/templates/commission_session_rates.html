How a booking finds its rate
Pack total first — every Awaken Force row exports with a credit of 1 no matter how big the pack is, so the billed total (₱9,600 = 8 × ₱1,200) is the only thing that tells one pack from another. Then the export's own session count, which is reliable for the Private Coaching packs. Then whatever single rate is left.
Leave Effective from blank for "as far back as there is data" and Effective to blank while a rate is current. Where ranges overlap the most recently effective one wins, so you can add a new rate without closing the old one first.
