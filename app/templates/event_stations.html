A note on the increment

It is the size of the button a coach taps, so pick it by how fast the work comes rather than by the maths. Burpees do not come in fives; wall balls do. A row is easier counted in hundreds of metres than in ones. Whatever you set here is what appears on the phone — +1 rep, +5 reps, +100 m — and the station closes itself the moment the target is reached.
